Mechanicrush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Information we collect

Mechanicrush does not collect any personal information. You do not need an account, and the game does not ask for your name, email, location or phone details.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, scrap coins, badges, settings and language) is stored only on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Ads and purchases

This version has no ads and no in-app purchases. If they are added later, this page will be updated to list the services used (e.g. Google AdMob, Google Play Billing).

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.
